## About me

I'm an Applied AI Engineer and Full Stack Developer with 3 years of experience and a Master's degree in Natural Language Processing. I build applications around LLMs, retrieval, AI agents, and document intelligence, covering the data pipeline, model integration, backend, frontend, and deployment.

My work includes production legal-analysis systems at **JURAI**, real-estate and geospatial data pipelines at **Paseetah**, and public models and datasets on **Hugging Face**. I have a particular interest in Arabic NLP, local model inference, and making complex documents useful through structured extraction and grounded answers.

- **Applied AI:** RAG, agent orchestration, LLM fine-tuning, semantic search, and conversational analytics.
- **Document intelligence:** OCR, document classification, layout-aware extraction, and synthetic datasets.
- **Data engineering:** Collection, cleaning, deduplication, validation, and resumable processing pipelines.
- **Full stack delivery:** FastAPI services, Next.js interfaces, PostgreSQL, Docker, and AWS.
